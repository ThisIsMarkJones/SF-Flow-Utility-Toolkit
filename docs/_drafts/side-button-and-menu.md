v7.2.0 reworks how you reach the toolkit: the side button can be hidden or moved, the menu is grouped and works from the keyboard, and the toolbar icon and **Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+U** open it from anywhere on the page.

## Hide or move the side button

In **Settings → General → Side Button**:

* **Hide the side button** keeps the ⚡ button off the page, so it doesn't appear in screenshots or screen recordings. Open the menu with **Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+U** or the toolbar icon instead.
* **Edge** puts the button and menu on the right or the left of the page.
* **Opacity when idle** fades the button to 75%, 50% or 25% when you're not using it.
* **Position:** drag the button up or down its edge, and it stays there. **Reset position** puts it back in the middle.

The side button also no longer appears on pages where the toolkit has no menu tools, such as the Flow details page.

## A better menu

* In Flow Builder, tools are grouped under **Canvas**, **Analyse**, **Assist** and **Move**, and a filter box at the top lets you type to find one.
* **Missing Description Flags** and **Setup Tabs** are a single row with a switch that shows whether they're on. While the flags are on, the **↻** on the same row refreshes them.
* Use the arrow keys to move through the menu, **Enter** to run a tool, and **Escape** to close it.
* The menu grows to fit its tools instead of scrolling.

## Toolbar icon and shortcut

Click the toolkit's icon in the browser toolbar to open the menu (pin it from the browser's extensions menu). On pages without toolkit tools, it opens Settings.

**Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+U** is now registered with the browser, so it works wherever focus is on the page — including inside Setup pages' frames — and you can change it in your browser's extension shortcut settings.

For details, see [Keyboard Shortcuts]({{ '/features/keyboard-shortcuts.html' | relative_url }}).
